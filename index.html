✦ PALAL
prayer · intercession · to stand in the gap

Palal is the Hebrew word for prayer — to intercede, to entreat, to bring before God.

It appears throughout the Hebrew Scriptures, from Abraham pleading for Sodom to Daniel confessing for his people.

The Messiah is referred to as: Yeshua יֵשׁוּעַ (Change this in Settings)
God is referred to as: Yahuah יָהוּה (Change this in Settings)
The Story of Redemption

From Genesis to Revelation, the story is one of redemption — God's call to His people, His faithfulness, and His promise of restoration.

In the Tanakh, we see the promise of a Messiah who would bring redemption. In the New Testament, we see this promise fulfilled in Yeshua.
